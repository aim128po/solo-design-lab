## AI Generation

The AI generation feature was the first thing I tried, and it was one of the most interesting parts of the experience.

I entered a short, fairly simple prompt describing the kind of business visual I wanted, and the tool produced a design in return.

What came back was relatively polished considering how little information I had provided. The layout made sense, the elements were positioned reasonably, and it gave me a usable starting point rather than something I immediately wanted to discard.

That said, "polished" doesn't mean "final."

The generated draft still benefited from a review pass. Some text needed tightening, and a few design choices were more generic than I'd want for a finished piece.

That's a normal part of working with AI-generated content rather than a criticism specific to Piktochart.

Where this feature seems genuinely useful is in solving the blank-page problem.

Starting a report or infographic from an empty canvas can be one of the slowest parts of the process. Having a reasonable first draft to react to and edit — rather than building everything from scratch — can save time, even if the output still needs a human review before it's ready to share or publish.
